# End-turn sound, round 3

> Internal. Checked 23 Nov 2026. **Nobody has listened to any of this.** Only levels and spectrograms were checked.

## Empire: Total War

Six text searches and two pages (forums, Reddit, video titles, mods, sound lists) found **no description** of its end-turn sound, and no audio was opened. So "same family" is a low-confidence judgement: 18th-century field music, real drums and brass. One word from the owner would help: drum, bell, bugle or cannon?

The last two are too peaky for -18 without crushing. Limiting was 4 dB at most. Candidates start within 10 ms and fade to 60 dB below peak; stems fade at least 33 dB. Only the kettledrum ends on a low boom, because all five rejected sounds ended on a low impact. The brass is B flat to A major, the Spanish cadence, written here from single recorded notes. All have light artificial echo.

**Ten stems** (`stem-*`, mono MP3 96): bell, bugle, field drum, kettledrum roll and strike, cymbal swell ([Brokenphono](https://freesound.org/people/Brokenphono/sounds/343827/), CC0), brass chord, ledger, stamp, low drum. Level -20 LUFS, or a peak near -4 dB for single hits (as low as -28.5), so a page that layers them needs a limiter.

## Not verified

- The fife tune (presumed traditional) is not identified.
- dobroide's band: only a drums-only passage is used; its location is unstated.
