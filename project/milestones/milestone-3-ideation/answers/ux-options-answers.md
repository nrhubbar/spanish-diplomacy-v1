# Owner answers to the Milestone 3 UX options page (2026-11-19)

Pasted by the owner from "Copy my answers" on ux-review/ (19 of 19).

## Readings of the mechanics answers
- Q-UX-I1 Units are the lasting pieces; an army is what leaves one territory with the same order that turn; men, morale and fatigue on units; no Organize order. **Yes.**
- Q-UX-I2 General attached to one unit. **No.** Note: "the general is a unit in and of itself, and its effect applies to the army it happens to be a part of."
- Q-UX-I3 Interception: passing units stop at the first entered transit territory and defend as a reinforcement; only the last step attacks. **Yes.**
- Q-UX-I4 Barricades (+1 d20) stack with Popular resistance (+3 d20). **Yes.**
- Q-UX-I5 One d20 weather roll a turn read against each zone's table; winter = Dec, Jan, Feb. **Yes.**
