### Q-RECR-1: Which recruitment model should Milestone 3 use?
  [x] Recruit and Replenish orders paid from resources (Option B) (rec)

### Q-RECR-2: Where may a faction recruit new armies?
  [x] Territories tagged for recruitment in scenario data, controlled and uncontested, one new army per territory per turn (rec)
  NOTES: Each territory should have a capital sub-territory. Recruitement can happen there. Recruitment requires: Money, Manpower, and certain buildings to be present. The "tagged can-recruit" in the scenario data is not needed; what's needed is the more provincal territory capitals should be missing the necesarry buildings to train proper regiments (e.g. only militia). A barracks enables infantry, a barracks and factory enable artillery, a factory and supply depot enable trucks, etc. 
    
    A player should be able to see all of the unit types, but some unit types should be greyed out and a hover will show what buildings or resources are missing.

### Q-PART-1: Which sub-regions should a territory split into?
  [x] 1936 provinces, with scenario overrides: Baleares as its three islands, Asturias in 2, Madrid in 3 (about 55 sub-regions) (rec)

### Q-PART-2: Should armies move between sub-regions, or should sub-regions only decide control and income?
  [x] Hybrid: orders and battles stay on the 18 territories; sub-regions decide control, cities, income and supply (rec)
  NOTES: I think movement must be based on sub-regions, otherwise we'll have to deal with funny issues like a surrounded army slipping through to friendly territory. Lets try this ruleset:
    1. Armies in contested territory by default attack for that territory. they can only attack or withdraw. they cannot entrench, or re-moraleize, or anything else.
    2. Withdrawing can only happen to an adjacent territory, and armies can only pass through friendly sub-territories to get there. This prevents that silly case from earlier
    3. You can only collect income/resources if you control the entire territory. You can only recruit/train if the territory is uncontested. Only militia will come to arms from within a contested territory if an order to call them was submitted when the territory became contested. Any other army will remain stuck. 
    
    These represent the chaos a region is under when contested, while also keeping the game's scope at grand strategy. I feel going full in on sub-territories makes it a little too tactical.

### Q-PART-3: When a graded battle result says k sub-regions change hands, which ones go?

  NOTES: I think I want automatic. Bascially defenders should strongly keep territories around the territory capital and weakly neighboring friendly territories, depending on how bad they lost. Setting up for a siege the defender should get a +1 die role for city barricades. For 3/N-way attack resolution, the defender is who ever controls the Capital, or had control of the territory last.

### Q-RAIL-5: Should the engine model track gauge now?

  NOTES: In our north star, we may want to treat rail conversion/gauge switching as a necesarry step in invading in a WW1/2 scenario.
